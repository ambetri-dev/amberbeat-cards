BTS won 6 awards at the Melon Music Awards 2026, including two grand prizes. The group took Artist of the Year and Album of the Year among the six trophies. The members sent their acceptance message by video rather than appearing in person. BTS won Artist of the Year at the Melon Music Awards 2026. The group also took Top 10, Best Male Group, Album of the Year. BTS delivered their acceptance message by video for the ceremony. The group had won Favorite Music Group for 4 straight years from 2020 to 2023, a record at the time. BTS broke their own all-time win record for that category in 2026.
