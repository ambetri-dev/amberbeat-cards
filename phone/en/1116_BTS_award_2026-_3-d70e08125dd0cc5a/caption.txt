BTS won a total of 6 trophies at the 2026 Melon Music Awards. The group also took Best Group Male, Top 10, Global Top 10. Stage of the Year, covering music, album, and performance categories. These wins came behind BTS's 5th full album, released about 3 years and 9 months after their previous one. The Stage of the Year trophy reflects the world tour that has been ongoing since April. BTS also won 3 categories at the 2026 Kids' Choice Awards, including Favorite Music Group. The title track from their album also earned Favorite Song at the same ceremony.
