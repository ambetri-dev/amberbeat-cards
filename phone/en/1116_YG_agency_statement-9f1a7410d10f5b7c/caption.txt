YG Entertainment said on Nov 16 that it is discussing group activities with BLACKPINK while members continue individual promotions. The agency said it will keep working on ways to show good activities under the BLACKPINK name. YG said it respects the members' wish to focus on individual activities for now. BLACKPINK debuted in 2016 and renewed its contract with YG Entertainment in 2023.
