I.N of STRAY KIDS released a solo digital single on Nov 17, 2026. He penned the lyrics and composed the melody of the synth-pop track himself, and also co-produced its video. The single depicts a clash between two egos, the existing self and a frenzied self. The release is the third entry in the group's solo project that began with Han's track in June. Felix's track followed in September, while the group kept releasing ‘Run It’. STRAY KIDS started a world tour in July that resumes in Hong Kong on Dec. 5. The tour covers 31 regions with 46 shows in total. STRAY KIDS will also release its fourth EP in Japan, ‘Suiatsu’, on Nov. 25. The group debuted in 2018.
