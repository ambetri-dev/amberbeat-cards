I.N is the third member to release music under the SKZ-PLAYER project, following Han and Felix.

I.N wrote the lyrics and composed ‘NO RETURN’, with Bang Chan of 3RACHA contributing to the track. I.N also took part in planning and directing the music video for ‘NO RETURN’.

I.N's digital single ‘NO RETURN’ topped the YouTube Music Video Trending Worldwide chart. The music video reached No. 1 on Nov 18, following its release on Nov 17.

The song is a synth-pop number about returning no matter how much one is pushed away.
