JENNIE's new track 'Power Trip' is featured on 'Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album.' The album was made by Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records.

JENNIE is the only Korean artist among the 34 tracks on the album. The track was released the same week as JENNIE's first Grammy nomination.

JENNIE debuted as a solo artist in Nov 2018.
