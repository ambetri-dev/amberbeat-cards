BTS won five trophies at the 2026 MAMA AWARDS, including Artist of the Year and Album of the Year.

The group also took Best Song Top 10, Best Male Group, and Fans' Choice. This was their 8th Artist of the Year trophy, the most in the category.

The win brought BTS's total Daesang (Grand Prize) count at MAMA to 24. BTS could not attend the ceremony because of their world tour schedule and sent a video message instead.
