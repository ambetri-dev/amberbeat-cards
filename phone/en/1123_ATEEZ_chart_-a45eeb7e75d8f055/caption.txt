ATEEZ's ‘구매 (Purchase)’ earned the group a 4th No. 1 on the Billboard 200.

ATEEZ has placed 10 consecutive albums in the Billboard 200 top 10 since 2022, the most among 2020s groups. Album sales made up about 97.9% of the first-week units for the project.

Streaming equated to about 4.04 million official on-demand plays of the tracks on the album. The album was released in 32 CD and vinyl versions including signed editions.

Photocards and posters served as collectible components alongside the physical formats. This stands as the most among 2020s groups on the chart.
