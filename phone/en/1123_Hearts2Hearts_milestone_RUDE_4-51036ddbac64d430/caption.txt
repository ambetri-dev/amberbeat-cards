TikTok challenge clips using member STELLA's English narration from ‘RUDE!’ passed 5.9 billion views, with over 6.35 million videos made using the song.

Hearts2Hearts' single ‘RUDE!’ passed 200 million Spotify streams as of Nov 19. The track is one of 4 Hearts2Hearts songs, including ‘The Chase’, ‘STYLE’ and ‘FOCUS’.

‘RUDE!’ is a house based dance song about free spirited, bold charm. It held No. 1 for 5 weeks on the Circle weekly streaming chart.

The song also earned 5 music show wins. More than 6.35 million videos have been made using the song.

Hearts2Hearts held their first local fan event ‘READY! S2U’ in Tokyo on Nov 22 and 23.

The event took place at Ariake Arena.
