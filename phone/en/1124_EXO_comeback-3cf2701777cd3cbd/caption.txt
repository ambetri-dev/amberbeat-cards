EXO-KAI Sehun will release their 1st EP ‘Waking Up The City’ on Dec 1, 2026.

KAI's solo track is a dark R&B song, while Sehun's solo track is a dance pop song built on 808 bass and percussion. A highlight medley video for the album was released through EXO's official channels on Nov 23 at 6 p.m. EXO-KAI Sehun will hold a showcase on Dec 1, the album's release day, to present the new songs on stage.

The album drops on Dec 1, 2026 and holds 7 tracks in total, including the title track, five unit songs.
