JENNIE and Tame Impala's 'Dracula' held at No. 3 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated Nov 28, 2026.

The track logged its 26th week in the chart's top 10. 'Dracula' also spent 46 weeks at No. 1 on Hot Dance/Electronic Songs.

The song also topped Radio Songs for 10 weeks, pulling in 58M in audience impressions. The official remix featuring JENNIE's vocals came out in February.

JENNIE has been credited on the chart since the April 4 chart. JENNIE is a member of BLACKPINK and also performs as a solo artist.
