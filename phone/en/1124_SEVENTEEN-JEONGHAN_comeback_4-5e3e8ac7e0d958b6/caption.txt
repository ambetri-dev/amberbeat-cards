SEVENTEEN's JEONGHAN will release a collaboration single called 'Afterglow' with Yamada Ryosuke of Hey!

Say! JUMP on Dec 14, 2026.

The single was announced on Nov 24, 2026, through Japanese label Storm Labels. JEONGHAN is currently on tour with fellow SEVENTEEN member JOSHUA, which began in Incheon on Oct 30, 2026.

The collaboration, titled ‘Afterglow’, was announced on Nov 24, 2026. The track is set to arrive on Dec 14, 2026.

‘Afterglow’ was written by Rose Blooming and Yoshi, with music composed by Yoshi and Joshua Lung. Joshua Lung also handled the arrangement of the track.

The song will be released for streaming and download at midnight Korea and Japan time.
