STRAY KIDS entered the Billboard 200 at No. 1 for 9 consecutive releases with their album ‘디스 앤드 댓 (This and That)’, which came out in August.

STRAY KIDS will kick off 2026 with their world tour ‘Run It Seoul Special’ at Gocheok Sky Dome on Jan 1-2. The group previously held 5 sold-out shows at KSPO Dome in Seoul in July and August as part of the same world tour.

The Gocheok Sky Dome shows expand the Seoul leg of their world tour to a bigger venue. The album was released in August.

STRAY KIDS headlined the music festival ‘Stray City’ across 3 regions in Latin America in September.
