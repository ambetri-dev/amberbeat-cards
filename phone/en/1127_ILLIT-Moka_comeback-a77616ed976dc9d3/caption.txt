ILLIT's Moka shared a letter with fans on Nov 27 announcing her plan to gradually return to activities.

Moka occasionally showed up at ILLIT events to cheer on the members. She said she plans to gradually resume activities together with her members.

She had briefly returned to some music show stages in late May. She paused activities again on June 19 due to anxiety symptoms.

ILLIT continued promotions as a four member group during that time.
